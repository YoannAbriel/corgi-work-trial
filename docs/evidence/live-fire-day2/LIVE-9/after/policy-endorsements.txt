About this screen
Prorated delta
The money that actually moved: the annual premium difference priced over the days remaining from the effective date to the end of the term, plus the state premium tax on it, rounded in the customer's favour. It is not the change in the annual premium, which is the last column.
A change in progress
The policy terms stay as they are until the delta is paid. Above $500.00 of additional premium in the term, the customer approves the quote from their own screen first.
Correcting a date
A correction reverses what was booked and re-books the endorsement on the right date. Nothing is deleted, and the money already collected stays where it is; the difference is collected or given back.Corgi · Policy administration

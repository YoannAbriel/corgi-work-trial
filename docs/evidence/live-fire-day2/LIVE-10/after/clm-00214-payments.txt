About this screen
The rail is a simulator
LOCAL SIMULATOR on every row: no bank is reached. Send is a business action; settle and return are controls of the simulated bank, and a real rail would send those events itself.
Paid counts from sending
A payment counts as paid from the moment it is sent on the rail. A return puts the money back and restores the reserve; it is a new entry, never an edit of the first one.
The same story in double entry
Setting a reserve, paying, settling and returning each append their own balanced entry. The position at the top of this screen is folded from the same events.Corgi · Policy administration

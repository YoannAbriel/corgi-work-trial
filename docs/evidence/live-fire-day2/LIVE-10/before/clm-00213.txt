About this screen
Incurred = paid + reserve
The figures are derived from this claim's events every time the page is rendered, never stored. The journal is the same story in double entry; if the two ever disagree, the page shows both rather than reconciling them for the reader.
Limits and approval
A payment is refused unless it clears the reserve available, the per-occurrence limit and the aggregate limit, and any payment above $1,000.00, counting what was already paid or asked for on this claim, needs a second person in the
[a /ops/approvals] money-out approvals queue.
The simulated rail
Paid counts a payment from the moment it is sent on the rail; the simulator settles it after 2 days or when a person presses settle, and a return puts the money back. A real rail would send those events itself.
Every rule is checked again
The server checks the role, the reserve available, both limits and the approval when a form is submitted, whatever this screen showed. A refusal comes back as a sentence at the top.
The bank check is a simulator
LOCAL SIMULATOR: a simulated ownership check, not a live bank integration. It says verified when the account holder name matches the claimant and the routing number is one this simulator can reach (110000000 or 110000005), and failed otherwise. Only the last four digits and a token are stored.Corgi · Policy administration

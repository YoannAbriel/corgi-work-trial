About this screen
Reading an entry
Credit: where the money comes from. Debit: where it goes. Every entry takes as much as it brings. The sentence under each entry's header is the one the posting stored when it was written.
Where a figure comes from
Each sum above is the addition of the journal lines of this policy; open one to see the lines it added and follow it to the entry that proves it. Nothing here is recomputed from the terms.
A correction never changes a row
It appends a reversal entry mirroring the original on the same effective date, and a re-booked event on the corrected date. Both stay in the journal for ever.
Refunds
A refund counts as completed only when Stripe's webhook confirms the money left. A failed refund reverses nothing: the customer is still owed the money.Corgi · Policy administration

About this screen
Incurred, reserve, paid
Incurred is what a claim has cost so far: paid plus the reserve still outstanding. A claim can be opened on a cancelled policy too, as long as the loss happened while the policy was in force.
Cancelling with a claim open
Cancelling never touches an open claim or its reserve. The refund covers unearned premium only, because the loss happened while the policy was in force.Corgi · Policy administration

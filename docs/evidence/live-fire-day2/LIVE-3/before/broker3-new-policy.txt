About this screen
What happens next
The draft is priced by the server and written as one immutable quoted event. The customer pays through Stripe, in test mode. The policy binds when the payment is confirmed and the verification is approved.
How it is priced
The premium tax comes from the effective-dated rate on file for the state chosen above. The policy fee is a flat $25.00, an assumption of this build and not a filed fee.
Where the arithmetic happens
On the server, in integer cents, when the draft is created. This form sends what you typed and computes nothing; the policy page shows the total it was given and how it was reached.
Binding
A draft can be created whatever the verification says. Binding is refused until the broker is approved, and money that arrives meanwhile sits in the suspense account until staff operations decide.Corgi · Policy administration

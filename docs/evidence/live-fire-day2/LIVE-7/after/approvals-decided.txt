About this screen
The threshold
Anything above $1,000.00 leaving this system, a claim payment or a cancellation refund, waits here until a staff approver who is not the person who asked says yes. The threshold is an assumption of this build, decided on 2026-09-08 and recorded in the decision log. It is not a regulatory figure.
It is cumulative
A payment below the threshold still waits here when it takes the claim’s money out above the line, and a refund below it waits when it takes the policy’s refunds above the line (lib/approvals/threshold.ts), so a payout cannot be split into sub-threshold pieces to skip the approver. That is why a small request can be in the list with no agent behind it.
Agent raised
The other sub-threshold case is a request an agent raised through the MCP endpoint, which waits for a human approver whatever the amount (rule 21 of the decision log). The person named in the row holds that key; an agent principal can never approve a money-out.
What you approve
Open a row to read it. What you approve is the exact text shown there, and its sha256 is stored with the request: at execution time the intent is rebuilt from the state of the world at that moment and the payment is refused if it differs by one character, so an approval can never authorise a different amount, claim or destination account.
Why a decision can be refused
Three reasons stop a decision, and the database enforces all three, not the screen. Not an approver: only a staff approver can decide, including against an automated agent. Your own request: maker-checker needs a second person, so the database refuses a decision whose author is the requester. Decided: somebody has already answered it, and a different amount, claim or destination account needs a new request.Corgi · Policy administration

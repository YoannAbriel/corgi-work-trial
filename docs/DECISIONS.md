## 2026-09-08T07:39:37+00:00 | Assistant proposal accepted by Yoann | Domain rules for v0

Commission basis: earned on collected premium, clawed back on refund, one rate per broker. Chosen because the required monthly statement lines are premium collected, commission earned, clawback and net due, so every line maps to a real ledger movement. Cancellation: pro-rata implemented; short-rate stays representable (calculation method stored on the cancellation event, dedicated penalty account). Rounding penny: the insurer eats it; customer charges are floored to the cent, customer refunds are ceiled to the cent, integer-cent arithmetic only. Term: end date is the same calendar date next year (Feb 29 maps to Feb 28), term days are the actual day count, premium earns daily on actual days. Taxes and fees: one US state modeled with an effective-dated rate table seeded with an official source URL and effective date, plus one flat policy fee; tax rides the charge, is excluded from earned premium and is refunded pro-rata with premium; the flat fee is fully earned at issuance. The state and its rate will be taken from an official source at implementation, never invented. Maker-checker thresholds are assumptions, not official rules: money-out (claim payments and refunds) above $1,000 requires a distinct human approver; endorsement additional premium above $500 requires customer approval. Auth: seeded demo users with a signed session cookie, roles broker, customer, staff_ops (maker) and staff_approver (checker).

## 2026-09-08T08:04:03+00:00 | Explicit user decision | Money rules decided by Yoann, superseding the assistant proposals of 07:39

Yoann asked that consequential domain and money rules be put to him with a plain explanation and a numeric example before being recorded, so that he owns and can explain each one (AF-06). The four rules previously recorded at 07:39 as "assistant proposal accepted" were re-opened as questions and decided by Yoann at about 07:58 UTC. Working rule from now on: the assistant explains options with a worked example, Yoann decides, the entry is written as his decision; purely technical choices stay labeled as assistant choices.

Common example: policy premium $1,200 written March 1, 2028, term to March 1, 2029 (366 days, 2028 is a leap year), cancelled after 100 days. Exact earned premium = 120000 x 100 / 366 = 32786.885 cents.

1. Rounding penny: the insurer eats it. Earned premium is floored (32786 cents), the customer refund is ceiled (87214 cents). Always in the customer's favor, one rule, deterministic. Rejected: customer eats the penny; round to nearest.
2. Term end: same calendar date next year, or the last day of February when that date does not exist. A policy written February 29, 2028 ends February 28, 2029; one written March 1, 2028 ends March 1, 2029 (366 days). Term days are always the actual count. Rejected: overflow to March 1.
3. Approval thresholds (assumptions of this build, not Corgi rules): money-out (claim payments and refunds) above $1,000 requires a distinct human approver, never the initiator and never an agent; an endorsement adding more than $500 of premium requires customer approval. Rejected: $500 / $250; approval on every amount.
4. Taxes and fees on cancellation: the state premium tax follows the premium and is refunded on the unearned portion; the flat policy fee is fully earned at issuance and never refunded. Example with 3% tax and a $25 fee on a $1,261 charge: refund = 87214 + 2617 + 0 = 89831 cents ($898.31), the tax refund being ceiled in the customer's favor. Rejected: fee also pro-rata; nothing refunded but premium.

Unchanged and already Yoann's decisions: commission earned on collected premium with clawback on refund; pro-rata cancellation with short-rate representable. The modeled state and its tax rate will be chosen from an official source at implementation and put to Yoann before being recorded.

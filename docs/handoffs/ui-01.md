## What changed

The existing home, login, broker, policy and operations pages share a cream-and-orange layout, responsive navigation, readable cards/forms and horizontally scrollable data tables. `PortalShell` receives the user already read by each page; it performs no authentication or data access. `WorkspaceOverview` is plain presentational JSX. Financial forms, hidden values, eligibility conditions, server queries and endpoints retain their existing contracts. Lucide React supplies icons.

The reference portal's existing overview was observed through a screenshot and accessibility read only. No click, navigation or form action was performed there. Its personal account content was not copied into the product or generation prompts.

Two decorative WebP illustrations were generated on the user-owned DGX Spark through its existing ComfyUI service and installed Krea model. Three generations were inspected: desk v1 rejected for its background, desk v2 and landscape v1 retained. The final assets total 31,698 bytes. [Artwork provenance](ui-01-artwork.json) records prompts, seeds, job IDs and final hashes. Artwork is not provider integration evidence.

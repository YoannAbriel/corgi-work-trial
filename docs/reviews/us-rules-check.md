## Consistent

- **US-01 premium tax rate.** 235 basis points matches the constitutional and RTC text. The refuter checked the code: the migration and the seed store a rate with its source URL and a checked-on date, and no screen asserts a legal scope. The 3 percent surplus lines rate falls on the surplus line broker under a different code section, so restricting this build to 2.35 percent is the ordinary reading, not an unsupported narrowing. Residual, not a finding: the scope sentence lives in DECISIONS.md, not in the seeded row's note column.
- **US-02 tax base.** Tax is computed on premium alone and never on the policy fee, and return premium is deducted pro rata on cancellation. The statute's express inclusion of home protection contract fees supports leaving an ordinary administrative fee outside the base.
- **US-03 tax on the customer bill.** No statute requires or forbids showing the tax as its own line. The separate line is recorded in DECISIONS.md as a design interpretation driven by the brief, not as a legal requirement.
- **US-06 pro rata refunds.** Pro rata is implemented as the statutory default. Short rate is representable in the chart of accounts but not computed, so no undisclosed non-pro-rata method is in use.
- **US-09 incurred and limits.** Incurred equals paid plus reserve, and both the per occurrence and the aggregate limit act as hard ceilings on a payment.
- **US-11 fiduciary premium funds.** The money never sits in a broker account, so no fiduciary holding arises. One wording caveat: the internal design language calls this agency bill and describes the broker as collecting and remitting, while the actual flow is direct to the insurer. The compliance position is sound, the term is loose.
- **US-13 endorsement proration.** The formula matches the standard days remaining over total days factor. The asymmetric rounding, floor on a charge and ceiling on a refund, is the recorded insurer eats the penny decision.

## Not modeled, to disclose

- **US-05 statutory cancellation notice.** The build validates only that the effective date sits inside the term, stores no cancellation reason, and generates no notice. The modeled path is a cancellation requested by the insured, which sits outside Ins. Code 676.2, but the staff operations path is the carrier side and would record a same-day cancellation with no notice trail. Week-two plan: add one line to COMPLIANCE-MATRIX.md or DECISIONS.md stating that statutory cancellation notice for California commercial policies, Ins. Code 676.2 and 677.2, is out of scope for v0 and that the recorded effective date asserts no lawful notice period.
- **US-08 claim handling timelines.** Claims carry occurrence, report and record dates but no acknowledgement event, no accept or deny decision event and no deadline column, so the 40 day decision clock and the 30 day payment clock have no stop point and cannot be derived. Nothing in the app claims Fair Claims Settlement Practices compliance. Week-two plan: model an acknowledgement and a decision event on the claim, derive the three clocks from them, surface the ageing on the claims screen, and disclose the gap in README until that lands.
- **US-10 producer licensing and appointment.** Binding and commission are gated on Stripe Connect business verification only. There is no licence lookup, no appointment record, no screening step and no disclosure in the README not-built list. The screens describe the check accurately as business verification, so nothing claims licensure. Week-two plan: record the producer licence number and the insurer appointment on the broker, check both before binding and before any commission is booked, and until then add producer licensing to the README list of things this build does not do.
- **US-12 sanctions screening of payees.** No screening exists on either money-out path, claim payment or premium refund, and the gap is not disclosed. Exposure in the artifact is nil because the system is sandbox only with seeded parties, no real money and no real personal data. Week-two plan: add an SDN screening step before a claim payment is sent and before a refund is issued, decide with counsel how a hit is handled, and disclose the absence in README in the meantime.
- **US-14 consent to electronic delivery.** Documents are rendered on demand and served to a signed-in owner, with no consent flag, no delivery record and no withdrawal path, and the README not-built list names e-signature but not electronic delivery consent. The activity log records who downloaded what, which is close in shape to the download evidence the statute wants, but it is explicitly diagnostic and fails open, so it is not a retention grade record. Week-two plan: store an opt-in consent with its disclosure text and timestamp, keep it for the retention period, gate document delivery on it, add a withdrawal path, and disclose the gap in README until that exists.

## Inconsistent

None. Both findings the checkers filed as inconsistent were refuted. US-01 was refuted on the code, on the record and on the legal premise, and US-05 was refuted on the scope of the statute and reclassified as a not-modeled scope gap.

## Unverifiable

- **US-04 flat policy fee under rate regulation.** The full text of Ins. Code 1860 and the definitions of rate and of a permitted policy charge could not be fetched. The build charges a flat 25 dollar fee, fully earned at issuance and never refunded, and the source that would confirm or deny that structure was not read. This is the highest-value open item in this record because it touches a figure the app charges today. Week-two plan: retrieve the statutory definitions and CDI guidance, or take a legal opinion, on whether an insurer-charged flat policy fee belongs in the filed rate, must be disclosed separately, and may be non-refundable on cancellation.
- **US-07 daily pro rata earning under SSAP No. 53.** The standard itself is not published free of charge and could not be fetched. Daily pro rata on actual calendar days is ordinary practice and nothing contradicts it, but the source was not read, so the check stands unverified rather than passed.
- **Partial.** US-08 was verified against a public mirror because the official publisher refused the fetch, and the checker's original attribution of the 15 day acknowledgement duty to 2695.7 was wrong; it sits in 2695.5. US-14's section 38.5 returns empty text on the official site and the rule now rests on 38.6 alone. US-09 rests on general public sources because SSAP No. 55 could not be retrieved.
